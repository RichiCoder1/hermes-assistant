# Code of conduct

Hermes Assistant is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free
community for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive, patient, and considerate.
- Discuss ideas and code without attacking the people involved.
- Respect different experiences, identities, and levels of expertise.
- Protect private information and remove secrets or personal conversation data
  from public reports.
- Accept moderator decisions and correction in good faith.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Responsible participation

AI-created and AI-augmented participation is welcome, but contributors remain
accountable for what they submit. Contributions must follow
[CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md), including its requirements for prior
discussion, author review, disclosure of material AI assistance, and readable,
focused submissions.

Maintainers may close low-effort, disruptive, unreviewable, or previously
undiscussed major submissions. Enforcing contribution quality and scope in a
consistent, respectful way is project moderation, not a Code of Conduct
violation. Disagreement with a technical or moderation decision does not excuse
harassment, personal attacks, or repeated disruption.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and other
project spaces. Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions,
reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when
needed to protect the community.

To report conduct privately, use the repository's
[private reporting form](https://github.com/RichiCoder1/hermes-assistant/security/advisories/new)
and begin the report title with `Code of Conduct`. Do not include unnecessary
personal information. Reports will be reviewed by the repository maintainer and
handled as confidentially as practical.
